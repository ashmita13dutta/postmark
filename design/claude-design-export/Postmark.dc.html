FROM · 24 SEPT
Ananya, to future Ananya
It rained in short shivers all afternoon, and the taxi at Elgin crossing wouldn't start. The driver laughed like he expected it. I ate jhalmuri in the doorway of a shut sari shop and watched the mustard-yellow cabs slide by like little suns.

Puja is a week away. The pandal near our building has begun to smell of bamboo and glue. I miss you already, today.

— A.
